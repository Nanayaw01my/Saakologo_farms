About Saakologo Farms

Based in Navrongo, Ghana, Saakologo Farms is the nation's leading agricultural solutions provider. For over a decade, we've been committed to delivering premium quality livestock and sustainable farming solutions to farmers across all 16 regions.

Our mission is simple: provide quality, reliable service, and expert support to help farmers succeed. We believe in growing agriculture and feeding our nation.
